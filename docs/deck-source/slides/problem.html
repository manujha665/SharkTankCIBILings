The problem

Credit intelligence arrives as a file, and it arrives late

Fragmented

Excel files, slicer dashboards, decks, batch outputs and custom studies. No single view of the story.

Static

Change one parameter, a state or a risk band, and it becomes a fresh request to the bureau team.

Late

Monthly files and quarterly decks describe a portfolio that has already moved on.

Unexplained

Benchmarks show that a lender differs from the market. They rarely show why, or what to do next.

03

Four words to remember: fragmented, static, late, unexplained. Keep it quick, one line per box. The judges live this problem inside the bureau, so say it from our side too: "We have some of the richest credit data in the country, and we still hand it over mostly as files and decks." Don't criticise any existing team or product by name. The point is that the delivery model hasn't caught up with the data.
