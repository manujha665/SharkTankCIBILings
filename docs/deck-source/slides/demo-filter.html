Working prototype · synthetic data · filters modelled in the prototype, from reported disbursal month and PIN code in production

08

A whole loan book mixes five-year-old loans with last month's, so a bad new policy can hide behind a good old book. Two new dropdowns fix that. Recent disbursements shows only loans made in the last 3, 6, 9 or 12 months, compared with the market's loans of the same age; young loans haven't had time to go 90 days late, so the platform adjusts for seasoning. PIN-code risk shows only borrowers in low, medium or high-risk PIN codes. Industry and peers are filtered the same way, so it stays like for like, and early-warning alerts always use the whole book. The story: on the whole book Sahyadri looks close to the market, but its personal loans from the last six months are at 3.38% against 2.78% for the market and 2.37% for peers, and the line breaks upward right after the February loosening. In high-risk PIN codes it is 7.41% against 5.54%. "It's the new loans." Both filters work on Portfolio Pulse, Industry Intelligence, Peer Benchmarking and the Logins Pulse (PIN-code risk only there, since logins are applications, not loans).
