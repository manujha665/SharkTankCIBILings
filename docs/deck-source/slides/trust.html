For a bureau, this slide can win or lose the room. Show the risks are designed out, not patched later. Tenant isolation: one lender can never see another's data, enforced below the screen. Peer privacy: a benchmark only appears if the peer group has at least five institutions and no single one is above 25% of it, so no one can be reverse-engineered. Governed AI: only approved LLMs, only approved datasets, a source on every answer. And an audit trail of every question, human or AI. All four already work in the prototype, on the Governance & Trust screen.
