Ticket size is the variable every dashboard forgets, and it comes straight from bureau data: the value disbursed and the number of new accounts each lender reports at opening, plus the loan amount stated in every enquiry. Nothing extra to submit. Sahyadri's average personal loan is ₹3.49 lakh against ₹2.59 lakh for the market and ₹3.14 lakh for private-bank peers. That gap alone could just be a metro, salaried customer base. The warning is that the gap is widest where risk is highest: near-prime and subprime tickets are 1.51 times the market's against 1.23 times in prime bands, and in Uttar Pradesh they went from ₹2.03 lakh to ₹2.87 lakh after the February loosening. Riskier borrowers and bigger loans means a higher loss on every default. The recommendation: a ticket cap by score band alongside the cut-off. Ticket size is a measure, not a separate page: flip Measure to Ticket size in the filter bar and the Command Centre, Industry Intelligence and Peer Benchmarking all switch over. It also raises its own early-warning alerts, and the AI analyst answers ticket-size questions.
