Land and expand. Standard at about ₹15 lakh a year gets every member onto the platform, and can be bundled with existing subscriptions. Pro at about ₹50 lakh is where the hero features and the weekly refresh sit, and where most revenue comes from: one avoided bad policy decision (₹0.45 crore a month in the demo) pays for it in about five weeks. Enterprise at about ₹2 crore is for large banks and NBFCs that want overlaps, their own data joined in, API access and a dedicated LLM tenant.
