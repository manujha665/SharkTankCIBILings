Two customers, two kinds of value. For the lender: a Pro licence at ₹50 lakh a year is earned back in about five weeks by one surgical policy decision like the demo's ₹0.45 crore a month. For us, an illustrative path: year one is the pilot, with three Pro and ten Standard members and a ₹4.9 crore investment. Year two reaches 60 Standard, 15 Pro and 3 Enterprise members, about ₹23 crore of revenue, and turns cumulatively positive. Year three reaches 120 Standard, 40 Pro and 10 Enterprise, about ₹60 crore of revenue against ₹12 crore of run cost, roughly an 80% contribution margin. Even 170 paying members is a small share of the bureau's member base, so the upside is larger.
