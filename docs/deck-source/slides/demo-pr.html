Today a Portfolio Review output lands as a spreadsheet and someone spends days reading it. Here, the lender presses one of four buttons (Consumer PR, Commercial PR, Microfinance PR, MFI + Consumer PR), uploads the file, and the platform maps the columns, validates every row and writes the story in seconds, from that file alone. On the sample Microfinance PR: Bihar's PAR 30 rose from 4.0% to 7.3%, borrowers with four or more MFI lenders carry 3.4 times the stress of single-lender borrowers, and ticket sizes are creeping up. The Commercial PR flags SMA-2 build-up (next quarter's NPAs) by sector and state; the Consumer PR finds the fastest-deteriorating product and state; the MFI + Consumer PR shows how stress spills across the two. This turns an existing bureau report into a recurring, self-serve product.
