Platforms like this fail when only the analytics team understands them. Every term and calculation in PortfolioIQ, 80 of them, is in a plain-English glossary: cure rate, roll-forward, SMA-2, PAR 30, basis points, mix shift, ticket size, PIN-code risk and more, each with how it is worked out. And nobody has to go there: hover over any dotted-underlined term on any screen, including the AI analyst's answers, and a short explanation pops up, then fades when you move away. "Open in glossary" jumps to the full card. This is what makes it usable by a CRO, a business head or a board member, not just a risk analyst. It also helps trust: every number's definition is one hover away.
