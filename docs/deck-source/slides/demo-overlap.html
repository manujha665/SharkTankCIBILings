This is the "wow, only we can do that" moment. A lender sees only its own borrowers; the bureau sees the same person across microfinance, retail and commercial credit. Of about 8 crore live MFI borrowers, half already show up in retail credit, and a quarter are servicing an MFI loan and a retail loan at the same time. Their retail delinquency is 2.7 times everyone else's. On the commercial side: in 41% of MSME defaults, a director or partner slipped on a personal loan or card first, about four months earlier. That's a risk signal and a growth pool at the same time (MFI borrowers with clean records are ready to graduate to retail). Be clear that the 8 crore, 50% and 25% are our working estimates.
