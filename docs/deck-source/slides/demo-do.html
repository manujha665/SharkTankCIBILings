"Insight without action is just a report." The simulator tests a policy before the lender commits to it. The instinct is to undo the cut-off change everywhere. The simulator shows that costs money, because the other states are fine. Tighten only in Uttar Pradesh and Gujarat, and net contribution goes up by about ₹0.45 crore a month. The phrase to land: "surgical, not blanket". This is also the number that proves value to the customer. New: the lender's real policy goes in first. They can type cut-offs for each of 10 products in each state, or import their policy file as CSV, and every number on the page, the recommendations and the AI analyst use that grid. The page also carries a feasibility note: on-screen entry and CSV import work today; a feed from the loan origination system comes in phase 2.
