This slide answers "when do we get our money back, and how sure are you?" Lead with the headline: the platform breaks even at about 32 paying members, and the plan has 170 by Year 3. So only about one in five of the planned members is needed to cover every cost. Why so few? Most of the cost is fixed: the team, the data refresh and hosting come to about ₹11 crore a year at scale, and the AI itself is tiny, about ₹6,000 per member a year. Each member brings in about ₹34 lakh of profit on average across the three tiers, so 32 members cover ₹11 crore. Timeline: Year 1 is the pilot year and loses about ₹4.9 crore, with only 13 members. From month 13 the platform is profitable every month, and by month 17, Year 2 month 5, it has earned back everything it spent. Then the stress test, before a judge asks: if we sign 30% fewer members and have to sell at the bottom of every price range, it still pays back early in Year 3 and makes about ₹18 crore over three years. One honest caveat if pushed: the model spreads each year's revenue evenly across the months; in reality members sign through the year, so true payback may come a few months later. All figures are team estimates from the Excel model, to validate with Finance.
