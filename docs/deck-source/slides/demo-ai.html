The question every judge will have: can we trust AI with bureau data? Answer it before they ask. The AI never calculates or invents a number. It chooses from a fixed set of approved tools, and every answer shows its source, filters and tool. Follow-ups keep context ("now compare that with Gujarat"). And when you ask it about HDFC, it refuses, because tenant isolation is enforced in the data layer, not just on the screen. It plugs into whichever LLM the organisation approves; with no LLM connected, it falls back to the governed engine, so a demo can't break. New: one flexible, governed query tool. Ask any combination of product, state, risk band, lender type, recent disbursements and PIN-code risk tier, and it compares you, the market and any lender type side by side. On screen: "Near-prime credit cards in Kerala, SFBs vs the market" (11.01% vs 8.31%, 270 bps worse), then "break that down by state". Invite a judge to make up their own combination: it shows the answers aren't scripted. And when the data can't answer, a district, a forecast, profitability, it says so first and shows "Not available" before giving the closest answer. Religion and caste are refused. You can also ask out loud with the mic. 79 ready-made questions.
