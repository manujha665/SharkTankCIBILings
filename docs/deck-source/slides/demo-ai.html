The question every judge will have: can we trust AI with bureau data? Answer it before they ask. The AI never calculates or invents a number. It chooses from a fixed set of approved tools, and every answer shows its source, filters and tool. Follow-ups keep context ("now compare that with Gujarat"). And when you ask it about HDFC, it refuses, because tenant isolation is enforced in the data layer, not just on the screen. It plugs into whichever LLM the organisation approves; with no LLM connected, it falls back to the governed engine, so a demo can't break. New: a mic button. A CRO can simply ask out loud, "how many applicants had more than one enquiry on the same day this month?", and get the governed answer.
