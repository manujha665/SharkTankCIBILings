Our moat

"Is it me or the market?" is a question only a bureau can answer.

It needs every lender's loans in the same product, state and risk band. We hold them. And every member that joins makes the benchmark sharper for all the others.

Pause before this slide and let it breathe. This is the line the judges should repeat in the room afterwards. Anyone can build a dashboard on a lender's own data. Nobody else can tell that lender how much of its problem is the market, because nobody else sees the market at segment level. Then the network effect: the more members use it, the better the peer benchmarks get, and the harder it becomes to replicate.
